## MARD palette data

`MARD_221_palette.json` contains 221 color-code/RGB entries selected from groups A–H and M of [`src/mard-color.json`](https://github.com/a31521424/pixel-to-beads/blob/main/src/mard-color.json) in [a31521424/pixel-to-beads](https://github.com/a31521424/pixel-to-beads).

The original BADI archive records source Git blob SHA `d16a4e6a9b21a2802929ce8bec9e765277e91b72` in `Palette_provenance.json`. The selected entries were serialized as a flat JSON mapping. This publication preserves the palette bytes from that archive.
